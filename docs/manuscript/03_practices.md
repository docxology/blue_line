# Practices

Working with the blue line is a discipline of declaration.

1. **Declare honestly.** A care signal is a declaration, optionally dated.
   Declaring a signal you did not perform corrupts the record
   the instrument exists to keep.
2. **State the cadence.** The registry can require a declared visit cadence;
   its presence is coverage of a declaration, not verified visits or neglect.
3. **Read the set-asides.** Intake notes are the instrument talking back.
   An unknown tag or an undatable date is drift you can fix.
4. **Retire in the record.** An ended commitment is marked ended where
   successors look, not silently dropped.
5. **Underclaim.** The verdict describes declarations. Say so.

The stewardship worklist lists commitment ID, missing or effectively stale signal,
and optional file-level declared owner, next-review date and source reference.
It is derived from the same surfaces as the verdict. Absent metadata stays absent;
the freshness window never manufactures a due date. No health score ranks
commitments, and no declared ownership discharges an obligation.
